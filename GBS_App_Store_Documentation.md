## App Description

### Short Description (150 characters)
A exclusive men's community platform for building meaningful connections, business networking, and personal growth.

### Full Description
The Good Blokes Society (GBS) app is the official mobile platform for members of the exclusive men's community founded in 2014. Originally started as a small gathering, GBS has grown into a thriving society that fosters lifelong friendships, meaningful business connections, and promotes men's health and wellbeing.

**Key Features:**
- **Member Directory**: Connect with verified members across Australia
- **Business Networking**: Discover and promote member-owned businesses
- **Exclusive Events**: Access to member-only events and gatherings
- **Secure Messaging**: Private and group messaging with fellow members
- **Special Offers**: Member-exclusive deals and discounts
- **Wellbeing Resources**: Mental health and lifestyle support
- **QR Code Check-ins**: Secure event attendance verification
