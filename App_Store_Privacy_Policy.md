# Good Blokes Society - App Store Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** March 25, 2026  

## Introduction

Good Blokes Society ("GBS," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use the Good Blokes Society mobile application (the "App").

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information
When you create an account and use our App, we may collect:
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, and password
- **Profile Information:** Profile photo, bio, location, professional information
- **Business Information:** Business details, services offered, contact information (if applicable)
- **Payment Information:** Payment method details (processed securely through third-party services)

### 2. Usage Data
We automatically collect certain information about how you use our App:
- **App Usage:** Features used, time spent, interactions with other members
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Location Data:** General location for event discovery and member matching (with your consent)
- **Communication Data:** Messages sent and received within the App

### 3. Automatically Collected Information
- **Log Data:** App access times, pages viewed, IP address
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies:** To enhance your experience and analyze usage patterns

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

### 1. Provide and Maintain Our Service
- Create and manage your account
- Connect you with other members
- Facilitate business networking and events
- Provide customer support

### 2. Improve Our Service
- Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
- Develop new functionality based on member needs
- Ensure App security and stability

### 3. Communication
- Send important updates about events and features
- Respond to your inquiries and support requests
- Send marketing communications (with your consent)

### 4. Safety and Security
- Verify member identities
- Monitor for fraudulent activity
- Enforce our Community Guidelines and Terms of Service

## Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

### 1. With Other Members
- Your profile information is visible to other verified members
- Business listings are visible in the directory
- Messages are shared with intended recipients

### 2. Service Providers
- Payment processors for transaction processing
- Cloud service providers for data storage
- Analytics providers for usage analysis

### 3. Legal Requirements
- When required by law or legal process
- To protect our rights, property, or safety
- To protect the rights, property, or safety of our members

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:
- **Encryption:** Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest
- **Access Controls:** Limited access to personal information
- **Secure Authentication:** Biometric and two-factor authentication options
- **Regular Security Reviews:** Ongoing security assessments and updates

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

### 1. Access and Correction
- View and update your personal information
- Request access to data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete information

### 2. Data Portability
- Request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format
- Transfer your data to another service provider

### 3. Deletion
- Request deletion of your account and personal data
- Remove specific information from your profile

### 4. Communication Preferences
- Opt out of marketing communications
- Manage notification preferences

## Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary:
- **Account Information:** Until you delete your account
- **Transaction Data:** As required for legal and financial purposes
- **Usage Analytics:** Anonymized data may be retained indefinitely
- **Legal Requirements:** Some data may be retained to comply with legal obligations

## Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete such information immediately.

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the updated policy in the App
- Sending email notifications for significant changes
- In-app notifications for policy updates

## App-Specific Privacy Features

### Biometric Authentication
- Face ID and Touch ID data is processed by your device only
- Biometric data is never stored on our servers
- You can disable biometric authentication at any time

### Location Services
- Location data is used only for event discovery and member matching
- You can control location permissions in your device settings
- Precise location is never stored - only general area information

### Messaging Privacy
- Messages are encrypted during transmission
- Message content is not used for advertising purposes
- You can report inappropriate messages or members

### Business Directory
- Business information is visible only to verified members
- You can control what business information is displayed
- Contact information is shared only with your explicit consent
